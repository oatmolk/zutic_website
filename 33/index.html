33 · Redes Valley

The real terrain around the 33 hectares near the Tanes reservoir in Caso, Asturias, inside the Redes UNESCO Biosphere Reserve. Inner area 6 × 6 km from the 5 m LiDAR elevation model with aerial photos at about 1.5 m per pixel, valley 20 × 20 km from the 25 m model, region to the coast 120 × 120 km from the 200 m model. Buildings are the real footprints and floor counts from the Spanish cadastre (Dirección General del Catastro, INSPIRE); roof shapes are simplified and roof colours come from the aerial photo. Individual trees are placed where the aerial photo shows forest canopy, with heights estimated; they are not surveyed trees. Village names from the IGN gazetteer (Nomenclátor Geográfico Básico). The bee museum (Museo de la Apicultura) is shown at the village of Tañes; its exact building is not marked.

Drag to orbit, pinch or scroll to zoom. Water flow, land use and sun hours are calculated from the terrain and are indicative only.
